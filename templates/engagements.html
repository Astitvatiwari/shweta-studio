Public Engagements

Art extending beyond the studio boundaries.

Through exhibitions, environmental campaigns, and community festivals, Shweta Jain Maheshwari fosters creative dialogue, sustainability, and cultural exchange.

Agra Art Festival

Founder & Director

Established the Agra Art Festival at Lalit Kala Sansthan to celebrate artistic expression and cultural dialogue. The festival connects local talent with the wider community, featuring live demonstrations and collaborative creative experiences with watercolor master Amit Kapoor.

Community Initiative

Maa Betiyaan

A collaborative visual arts project exploring family, identity, creativity, and intergenerational connection. Through drawing and fine-line compositions, the initiative encourages participants to share and celebrate their personal stories.

Environmental Campaign

Art for Nature, Action for Future

An educational and social engagement initiative using art to advocate for sustainability. Through workshops and public art installations, participants repurpose discarded and studio materials, prompting active dialogue around environmental responsibility.

Press & Recognition

Dainik Jagran & Civic Support

Shweta’s work and community initiatives have received broad civic support and regional media coverage, including inaugurations by civic leaders like Shri Naveen Jain, Member of Parliament (Rajya Sabha), acknowledging the role of art in community building.
