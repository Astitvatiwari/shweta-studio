About this Work

Drawing V is a fine-line ink study and charcoal wash composition on archival paper. The artwork is part of the artist's ongoing exploration of forest landscapes, roots, and network systems in nature. Shweta Jain Maheshwari meticulously layered thin ink lines to represent interlocking roots, while soft charcoal washes create a sense of misty, atmospheric depth. The concept represents connection—how natural systems, trees, and earth are silently bound beneath the surface, reflecting our own interconnectedness as a human community. The subtle detail and calm grey values make this a meditative work that invites close inspection.

Every artwork by Shweta Studio is handcrafted individually. Variations in texture, surface, and finish are a natural part of the artistic process and contribute to the uniqueness of each piece.
