About this Work

Artistic Jewellery V is a large, teardrop-shaped botanical pendant containing delicate pressed baby's breath flowers and gold leaf flakes cast in high-clarity UV resin. The design is inspired by the stars in a night sky, with the tiny white flowers representing distant stars and the gold flakes representing stellar dust. Shweta Jain Maheshwari carefully arranged the fragile flowers to prevent them from bruising during the casting process. The resulting piece has a highly delicate, romantic presence, making it a beautiful collector's item and wearable art work.

Every artwork by Shweta Studio is handcrafted individually. Variations in texture, surface, and finish are a natural part of the artistic process and contribute to the uniqueness of each piece.
